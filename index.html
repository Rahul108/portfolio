What I've built and fixed

Payments, lending and payout systems. I started in QA and now lead a platform rewrite.

  1. Senior Software Engineer · Optimization Team

    Miaki Media Ltd (on assignment at Grameenphone)

    Aug 2024 – Present

    Payment platform rewrite: PHP to Go and NestJS

    • Lead the rewrite. I plan the phases, mentor the team, and work with the delivery team so the business stays on track.
    • Rewrote the core, recharge and roaming services in Go. I picked Fiber after testing Gin and Chi. Roaming is live, and core and recharge are close to full rollout. They use about half the resources in load tests.
    • Rewrote the shared product catalog in NestJS. It now handles 600+ TPS under 50 ms in production, up from 200+. Product changes take 2 days instead of 7–10.
    • Added tracing and structured logs to 6 services. The worst bugs now take under an hour to trace, down from 7–8 hours.
    • Write the spec and tests first, then build with AI tools. Features now ship in about half the time, with far fewer validation bugs.

    Performance & reliability

    • Cut Redis memory by ~60% on a recharge service with millions of daily transactions. It now stores shared product IDs instead of a full product copy for every user. Redis stopped running out of memory.
    • Found the cause of 4 critical bugs that had been open for 4 months. Load tests on Kubernetes showed it was connection pooling in Redis and the database.
    • Replaced hourly cron jobs with real-time data streaming (CDC). I compared Debezium, Flink and Maxwell with the data team, then built the service that uses it for notifications and reconciliation.
    • Moved payment notifications and refunds to RabbitMQ. The main API got ~20% faster. Refunds used to time out above ~100 TPS and now handle 600+ in load tests.
